## Source boundaries

The formalization incorporates work directly relevant to RES=RAG and CSNP:

- optimal-transport interpretation of the receptive/generative relation;
- history-dependent informational substrate and dual operators;
- dialogical-time and `T_Real` lineage;
- measurable interface variables for constraint density, propagation rate,
  and governance capacity;
- bounded, non-zero equilibrium and explicit falsifiers;
- signed temporal displacement and phase-labelled interaction dynamics;
- CSNP protocol lineage, deterministic receipts, and audit/replay rules;
- endogenous-stability and governability concepts;
- the multi-task braking proposal as a falsifiable intervention; and
- epistemic separation of reported numerical claims from operational control
  parameters.

It does not import unrelated projects or treat stronger physical, ontological,
or security claims as established merely because they appear in an antecedent
source.

## Research-claim boundary

The v1.1 paper distinguishes definitions and protocol rules from reported
claims. In particular, 0.42, 0.66, and approximately 2.14 are not claimed as
universal constants by the v1.1 formalization. They are carried as
source-attributed research claims unless validated under a declared metric
profile.

## Terminology provenance

Version 1.1.0 continues to use **Coherent State Network Protocol** as the
canonical expansion of CSNP. The earlier **Client-Side Narrative Protocol**
term, associated with Mohamad Al-Zawahreh and the ARK/CSNP research lineage, is
preserved as an optional sovereign-memory implementation profile.
