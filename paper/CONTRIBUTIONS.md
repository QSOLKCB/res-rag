## Source boundaries

The formalization incorporates only work directly relevant to RES=RAG:

- optimal-transport interpretation of the receptive/generative relation;
- a history-dependent informational substrate and dual operators;
- dialogical-time and `T_Real` lineage;
- measurable interface variables for constraint density, propagation rate,
  and governance capacity;
- bounded, non-zero equilibrium and explicit falsifiers;
- CSNP protocol lineage, deterministic receipts, and audit/replay rules;
- endogenous-stability and governability concepts; and
- ethics and safety implications already present in the RES=RAG programme.

It does not import unrelated projects or claim results from work that does not
bear directly on RES=RAG or CSNP.

## Terminology provenance

Version 1.0.0 uses **Coherent State Network Protocol** as the canonical
expansion of CSNP. The earlier **Client-Side Narrative Protocol** term,
associated with Mohamad Al-Zawahreh and the ARK/CSNP research lineage, is
preserved as an optional sovereign-memory implementation profile.
