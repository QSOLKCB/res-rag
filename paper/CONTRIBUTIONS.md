## Source boundaries

The formalization incorporates only work directly relevant to RES=RAG:

- optimal-transport interpretation of the receptive/generative relation;
- a history-dependent informational substrate and dual operators;
- measurable interface variables for constraint density, propagation rate, and
  governance capacity;
- bounded, non-zero equilibrium and explicit falsifiers;
- CSNP protocol engineering, deterministic receipts, and audit/replay rules;
- ethics and safety implications already present in the RES=RAG specification.

It does not import unrelated QSOL-IMC projects or claim results from work that
does not bear directly on RES=RAG.

## Terminology provenance

Version 1.0.0 uses **Coherent State Network Protocol** as the canonical
expansion of CSNP. The earlier term **Client-Side Narrative Protocol** is
preserved as the name of an optional sovereign-memory implementation profile.
